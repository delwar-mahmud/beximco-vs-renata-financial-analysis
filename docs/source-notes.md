## Specific interpretation issues

1. **Currency scale:** both company input worksheets display “In BDT Thousands”, while entered financial statement values have the magnitude of full BDT amounts. Confirm the original annual report units before presenting absolute revenue, profit or asset figures. The overview uses ratios to avoid assigning an unverified currency scale. Ratio comparability still depends on consistent units within each calculation.
2. **Debt terminology:** the workbook's debt-to-equity and debt-to-assets formulas include all current and non-current liabilities. The overview labels these as total-liabilities ratios.
3. **Renata revenue reconciliation:** the revenue segmentation total exceeds reported turnover by 2,020 for 2020 and 2,021 for 2021 in the workbook's entered units. Net profit margin calculations use reported turnover. The source figures require reconciliation before relying on the segmentation totals.
4. **EPS:** the workbook uses restated EPS and consistent share counts across the three years for each company. Verify the corporate action and restatement basis against the annual reports before drawing share-price conclusions.
5. **Strategic claims:** regulatory benefits, export incentives, vaccine activity, credit ratings and other historical claims come from the presentation. They require their original dated citations for formal publication.
6. **Benchmark scope:** the supplied financial comparison covers two companies. It does not establish an industry-wide benchmark or prove that specific strategic actions caused the observed results.

## Verification performed for this portfolio

Selected net profit margin, ROCE, asset turnover, current ratio, operating cash flow and total-liabilities ratios were checked against the workbook inputs and formulas. The CSV and overview use the saved workbook results. The original workbook was not edited or recalculated.
